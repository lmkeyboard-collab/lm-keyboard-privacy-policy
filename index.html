01 · Overview

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LM Keyboard (Lamaing Keyboard) handles information when you install or use the LM Keyboard mobile application, its keyboard component, and related features on Apple iOS or Google Android devices.

We believe a keyboard should be clear about what leaves your device. This policy distinguishes between ordinary typing, on-device dictionary features, and features that may require a network connection.

02 · Availability

Platform Support

LM Keyboard is available for both Apple iOS and Google Android devices. This Privacy Policy applies equally to both versions unless otherwise stated.

03 · Collection

Information We Collect

LM Keyboard is designed to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Depending on the features you choose to use and how the app is configured, information processed may include:

  • App settings and preferences, such as selected language, theme, keyboard layout, font choices, animation/effect settings, Mon typing preferences, Myanmar Helper Keys preferences, and dictionary settings. These preferences are generally stored locally on your device or in shared app/keyboard storage so the containing app and keyboard extension can use the same settings.
  • Dictionary activity, such as saved words, favorites, or search history, if these features are enabled.
  • Test Keyboard notebook content, such as pages and text you type in the in-app Test Keyboard notebook. This content is stored locally on your device for the feature to work and is not treated as ordinary keyboard input sent to an online service.
  • Text you actively submit to an online translation or other network-based feature.
  • Dictionary contributions you deliberately submit, such as meaning corrections, suggested new words, comments, language fields, and optional pronunciation/voice recordings. These submissions may be sent to LM Keyboard’s backend for review and dictionary maintenance.
  • A Google Gemini API key you choose to provide for AI translation. If you select “Save Key,” the key is saved in LM Keyboard’s local app storage so it can authenticate Gemini API requests that you initiate. It remains there until you delete it in the app or remove the app.
  • Photos you deliberately select when creating or changing a keyboard theme. LM Keyboard accesses only the photos you select through the platform’s permission or photo-selection interface and uses them to provide the requested theme.
  • Basic technical information, such as app version, operating-system version, device category, diagnostic events, and crash information, only if diagnostic features are used.
  • Support communications you send to us, including your message and contact information.

LM Keyboard does not sell personal information or use keyboard input, translated text, dictionary content, or clipboard content for behavioral advertising. We limit any processing to information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for a disclosed feature, support request, advertising function in the main app, security purpose, or legal obligation. Where the GDPR applies, personal information is processed only when an appropriate lawful basis applies, such as consent, performance of a requested service, compliance with law, or a legitimate interest that is not overridden by your rights.

04 · Typing

Keyboard Input

Ordinary keyboard input is processed to provide typing functionality. LM Keyboard does not record, store, or transmit everything you type merely because the keyboard is active.

Apple iOS and Google Android apply their own protections to third-party keyboards, secure text fields, permissions, and app data. On iOS, Full Keyboard Access must be enabled manually to use AI translation and online dictionary updates. It is not required for basic typing. On Android, the keyboard is enabled and managed through the device’s language and input settings.

Text leaves your device only when you deliberately use a feature that requires online processing, such as AI translation, an online dictionary request, or a dictionary contribution you choose to submit. Toolbar dictionary search text and typing preferences are processed only as needed to provide those features and are not used to build an advertising profile. See “Dictionary Features,” “AI Translation,” and “Permissions” below.

05 · Language tools

Dictionary Features

LM Keyboard may provide word lookup, suggestions, favorites, recent searches, or user-added dictionary entries. Where possible, these features operate locally on your device or through storage protected by the security controls of iOS or Android and accessible only to the relevant LM Keyboard app components.

If a dictionary lookup requires an online service, the word or phrase you deliberately submit and the minimum technical data needed to return the result may be transmitted to the service. LM Keyboard does not transmit unrelated keyboard input as part of a dictionary lookup.

If you deliberately submit a dictionary report, corrected meaning, new word, comment, or optional pronunciation/voice recording, LM Keyboard may send that submission to its backend for review by an administrator. Approved dictionary content may later be synchronized to LM Keyboard dictionary distribution sources so it can be made available to users. Do not include unrelated personal or confidential information in dictionary submissions.

06 · Online processing

AI Translation

LM Keyboard offers optional AI translation through the Google Gemini API. When you intentionally request an AI translation, the selected text or text you submit is sent to Google over the internet together with the technical information needed to authenticate, process, and secure the request.

To use this feature, you may provide your own Google Gemini API key. LM Keyboard uses the key only to authenticate Gemini API requests that you initiate. If you select “Save Key,” the key is saved in LM Keyboard’s local app storage and remains there until you delete it in LM Keyboard or remove the app. Treat the API key like a password, restrict it to the Gemini API where possible, and delete or replace it immediately if you believe it has been exposed.

Google’s handling of submitted text and generated translations depends on the Gemini service tier associated with your API key. Under Google’s current terms, content submitted through unpaid services may be used to improve Google products and may be reviewed by human reviewers. Paid-service content is handled under different terms. Do not submit passwords, payment details, health information, private keys, confidential material, or other sensitive or personal information for AI translation.

LM Keyboard requires an intentional user action before transmitting text for AI translation and uses encrypted network connections in transit. AI translation can be disabled, and local-only translation mode can be selected where available. For more information, review the Gemini API Additional Terms, Gemini API key guidance, and Google Privacy Policy.

07 · Device access

Permissions

Depending on your platform and the features you choose, LM Keyboard may request limited access to device capabilities. A permission is requested only when it is needed to provide a requested feature.

  • Full Keyboard Access (iOS) is requested only when you choose to use AI translation or online dictionary updates that require network access or communication with the containing LM Keyboard app. Basic typing remains available without Full Keyboard Access.
  • Internet access is used for AI translation through Google Gemini, online dictionary updates, Google AdMob advertising in the containing app, and related security functions.
  • Photos, optional, may be requested when you choose a photo for a custom keyboard theme. LM Keyboard uses the selected photo to create or display that theme and does not use it for advertising or AI translation. You can change or revoke photo access in device settings.
  • Microphone, optional, may be requested when you choose to record and submit a pronunciation or voice contribution. The microphone is used only for the recording action you initiate; LM Keyboard does not continuously record audio in the background.

You can review or change permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related optional feature from working, but permissions are not used for unrelated purposes.

08 · Operating systems

Platform Differences

Apple iOS and Google Android use different security models, permission systems, keyboard-extension frameworks, advertising controls, and device settings. As a result, the name, timing, or availability of a permission or feature may differ between platforms.

LM Keyboard follows the same privacy principles on both platforms: data minimization, clear purpose, user choice, secure handling, limited retention, and no sale of personal information. Platform-specific behavior is described in this policy where relevant. Our disclosures and practices are intended to align with applicable Apple App Store Review Guidelines, the Google Play User Data policy, the GDPR, the CCPA, and other applicable privacy laws.

09 · Advertising

Advertising

LM Keyboard uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements in the main or containing mobile application and to help support development and maintenance. Advertising is not displayed inside the keyboard extension or keyboard interface. The content you type, text submitted for translation, dictionary content, and clipboard content are not provided to Google AdMob for selecting or personalizing advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; device and advertising identifiers; app and ad interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views; advertisements shown to you; and diagnostic or performance information. Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, perform analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, improve its advertising services, and limit how often an advertisement is shown.

Advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law. Where required, LM Keyboard will request consent or present privacy choices before requesting ads. On iOS, LM Keyboard will use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking across other companies’ apps or websites when permission is required. Refusing tracking or personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all advertisements; contextual or non-personalized advertisements may still be displayed.

You can reset or delete the advertising identifier using the privacy or advertising controls available in your device settings. Where required, LM Keyboard will also provide a way to review or change applicable advertising privacy choices. For more information about Google’s data practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

10 · Providers

Third-Party Services

LM Keyboard uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, for advertising and related measurement, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention functions. Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

LM Keyboard uses the Google Gemini API for user-requested AI translation. Google may receive the text deliberately submitted for translation, the generated response, authentication information associated with the user-provided API key, and limited technical information needed to provide and secure the service. Processing is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

The external services currently identified in LM Keyboard include Google AdMob for advertising and the Google Gemini API for optional AI translation. LM Keyboard’s dictionary contribution and synchronization workflow may also use developer-controlled services and repositories, including Google-hosted backend/storage services, GitHub, and Hugging Face, to review, store, synchronize, or distribute approved dictionary and pronunciation data. Only information you deliberately submit for those dictionary features should be included in that workflow. LM Keyboard does not use a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. Our Apple App Store privacy disclosures, required privacy manifests, Google Play Data Safety disclosures, and actual app behavior are intended to remain consistent with this policy.

12 · Lifecycle

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant feature, meet security and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Locally stored settings, Test Keyboard notebook pages, and dictionary data generally remain on your device until you delete or reset them, reset the app, or uninstall it. Dictionary reports, suggested words, comments, or voice contributions that you deliberately submit may be retained for review, moderation, synchronization, dictionary maintenance, and recordkeeping.

A saved Google Gemini API key remains in LM Keyboard’s local app storage until you delete it in the app or remove the app. A photo selected for a custom keyboard theme remains on the device as part of the theme until you replace or delete the theme or remove the app. Google AdMob and Google Gemini may retain information in accordance with their own published policies, service tier, and legal obligations. When information controlled by LM Keyboard is no longer reasonably needed, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible. You may contact us to request deletion of personal information controlled by LM Keyboard, subject to applicable legal, security, and technical limitations.

13 · Young users

Children’s Privacy

LM Keyboard is not intended to collect personal information from children without any consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

If LM Keyboard is made available to children or participates in Apple’s Kids Category or Google Play Families program, advertising and data practices will be configured to meet the applicable child-directed requirements, including restrictions on personalized advertising and third-party SDKs.
